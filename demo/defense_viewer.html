    Evidence limits

    IPPO and MAPPO did not beat MPC under this fixed training budget. The full MPC gain is concentrated in two of 44 episodes, both starting at 04:00; 42 episodes tie idle sharing. Terminal resets prevent carrying energy across four-hour block boundaries. June had already been inspected in the allocation pilot. MPC uses community observations and continuous controls; learned actors use local observations and discrete requests. CVaR is diagnostic, not a daily risk constraint. Annual payback and live-grid performance are unproven.
