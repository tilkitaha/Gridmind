\section{Justification of the topic}
Why the topic was chosen. Write in the impersonal form, e.g. ``the analysis
was carried out'', never ``I'' or ``we''.

\section{Objective and scope of the thesis}
The objective, formulated so that it can be measured, and the scope
(subject, object and period of the research).

\section{Research problems}
The theoretical and/or empirical problems to be solved.

\section{Concept, methodology and assumptions}
The general concept of the thesis, the research methodology and the
assumptions adopted.

\section{Structure of the thesis}
Chapter 2 presents \ldots{} Chapter 3 \ldots{} Chapter 6 concludes the thesis.

\section{Division of work}
A team thesis states the contribution of each co-author (7.2).
